# Security reporting policy — draft, not adopted

The maintainer must select and verify a private reporting channel before adopting
a SECURITY.md policy. No email address, response SLA or supported-version commitment
has been established. Do not post credentials or exploitable details in public issues.

Proposed report contents: affected version, a minimal synthetic reproduction, impact,
and the relevant filesystem or trust boundary. Remove private paths and secrets.
Publish the final policy only after the maintainer confirms the contact route.
